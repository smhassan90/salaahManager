How to Install the App

  1. Open Google Play Store
    On your Android phone, tap on the Play Store app icon.
  2. Search for the App
    Tap the search bar at the top and type: "AlAsr Manager Masajid Prayer Timings" or search for "AlAsr Manager"
  3. Identify the App
    Look for the app with the mosque icon (🕌) shown above. Make sure it's the correct app before installing.
  4. Install the App
    Tap the "Install" button and wait for the installation to complete.
🔍 Finding Your Masjid
After opening the app, search for: "Ukasha Masjid"
🕌 Ukasha Masjid 🕌

Add to Home Screen

  1. Open the App
    Launch the AlAsr Manager app from your app drawer.
  2. Find Your Masjid
    Search for and select "Ukasha Masjid" from the masjid list.
  3. Add to Home
    Tap the "Add to Home" button or use the menu option to add Ukasha Masjid to your home screen for quick access.
